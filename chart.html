Morse code chart

Every character, drawn to scale. Tap one to hear it. Use this to check a letter after a drill, not to learn from. Your ear learns faster than your eyes.

Letters

A dah is three times as long as a dit.

Numbers

Always five sounds. 1 to 5 start with dits, 6 to 0 with dahs.

Punctuation

The period and comma are six sounds each.

Prosigns

Two or more letters sent as one, with no gap between them.

The short codes nearly every contact uses.
